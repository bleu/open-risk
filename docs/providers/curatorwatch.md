# CuratorWatch

CuratorWatch is a free public dashboard providing "on-chain curator & vault intelligence" for DeFi lending vaults, with data refreshed roughly every 6 hours. It grades vaults and the entities ("curators") that manage them, surfacing TVL, yield, fees, liquidations, and a set of statistically-triggered risk alerts — originally Morpho-centric and expanding to other vault protocols via the Turtle API.

## Coverage of Top 20

CuratorWatch is a vault-curator monitor. Its native subject is **Morpho** and **Morpho Vaults** (curated MetaMorpho vaults), which is exactly the "curator" model it grades. Its changelog indicates cross-protocol expansion to other vault-bearing lending protocols (Aave, Euler, Compound, Spark) sourced through the Turtle API, though Morpho remains the primary, deepest coverage.

- **Likely covered:** Morpho, Morpho Vaults (primary/native). Possibly partial: Aave, Euler, Compound, Spark — the changelog lists these as integrated via the Turtle API, but depth/confirmation per-protocol could not be verified from public surfaces.
- **Not applicable:**
  - Uniswap, Curve, Balancer (DEX/AMM), CoW Swap, 1inch, 0x / Matcha (swap aggregators) — no curator-managed lending vaults to grade.
  - Lido, Rocket Pool (liquid staking) — issue LSTs, not curated vaults; CuratorWatch's curator/vault grading model does not apply.
  - Fluid, Gearbox, Liquity, Spark (as a CDP/lending protocol rather than a vault host), Pendle, Yearn, Mellow — these are lending/yield protocols but are not built on the curator-managed vault model CuratorWatch grades, unless they appear via Turtle's cross-protocol feed. Yearn and Mellow are vault systems but were not confirmed in CuratorWatch's coverage.
- **Notes:** Coverage was inferred from the homepage tagline, the /docs methodology (which references "Morpho" and "Turtle" protocol filters), and the changelog (which lists Morpho, Turtle, Aave, Euler, Compound, Spark via the Turtle API). No live protocol-list enumeration was performed (documentation-only). The strongest, most reliable coverage is Morpho / Morpho Vaults; treat broader lending coverage as unconfirmed/partial.

## Data products & meaning

### Curator & vault grading
- **What it is:** A grading layer over each active vault and curator. Vaults are bucketed into High / Medium / Low Grade based on **9 hard requirements** (e.g. "Vault TVL >= $1 million", "Curator has legal entity", "Curator operating time >= 6 months", "Curator total AUM >= $10 million", "Curator bad debt: Zero", "Vault bad debt (liquidations): Zero", "APR / APY <= 20%", "Institutional collateral >= 80% blue-chip assets", "Vault age >= 90 days"). All vaults also get a **composite 0–100 risk score** across five factors: Size (0–25), Maturity (0–15), Curator (0–35), Collateral Quality (0–15), Risk Indicators (0–10).
- **What it means:** A normative quality/safety judgment of who is managing depositor capital and how conservatively. The "Curator" factor weights legal-entity status, operating history, AUM, and bad-debt history — i.e. operational/counterparty trust, not just on-chain mechanics.
- **Shape / fields:** Per-vault and per-curator records: grade (High/Medium/Low), 0–100 score, the five factor sub-scores, the 9 hard-requirement pass/fail flags, TVL, APR/APY, collateral tier (Tier 1 major stablecoins / Tier 2 blue-chip crypto / Tier 3 regulated stablecoins), curator legal entity type, operating duration, AUM, bad-debt history.

### Yields lens
- **What it is:** Yield generation across vaults and curators.
- **What it means:** Comparative APR/APY across curated vaults, normalized for cross-protocol comparison (APR/APY conversion was corrected for multi-protocol consistency per changelog).
- **Shape / fields:** Per-vault APR/APY, TVL, curator, protocol badge.

### Fees lens
- **What it is:** "Estimated fees generated by Morpho vaults and curators."
- **What it means:** Curator revenue / performance-fee economics — how much each curator extracts.
- **Shape / fields:** Per-curator/per-vault estimated fee revenue (exact columns not documented publicly).

### Liquidations lens
- **What it is:** Liquidation events across curated vaults, with bad-debt analysis.
- **What it means:** Realized loss / collateral-health events at the vault level, feeding the "zero bad debt" hard requirements.
- **Shape / fields:** Per-vault liquidation events and bad-debt figures (exact columns not documented publicly).

### Alerts
- **What it is:** Statistically-significant event monitoring (events that occur <5% of the time). Types: APY change >20% vs 7-day avg; deposits/withdrawals >10% of vault TVL; vault TVL drop >10% in 24h; curator AUM drop 5%/24h or 15%/3d; ecosystem AUM drop 3%/24h. Filterable by type (Vault/Curator/Ecosystem), severity (Critical/Warning/Info), and window (24h/7d/30d).
- **What it means:** Real-time behavioral/risk signals on curator and vault activity.
- **Shape / fields:** Alert records with type, severity, target (vault/curator/ecosystem), timestamp.

### LP Return Calculator
- **What it is:** A tool projecting depositor returns given deposit amount, vault grade, collateral type, protocol/asset/curator/APR filters.
- **What it means:** Utility/UX, not a distinct importable risk feed.

**Most relevant to import:** The **curator & vault grading** product (grade + 0–100 score + 9 hard requirements). It is the verbatim, structured "what this feed says" output that best fits the aggregator's per-protocol feed cards, and it is the unique signal CuratorWatch offers (curator operational/counterparty quality). The Alerts stream is a strong secondary import for the monitoring dimension.

## Metric alignment

- **Ethereum-principles signal:** Partial. The "Curator" factor and the legal-entity / operating-history / governance-of-allocation framing touch on who controls depositor capital and counterparty/operator trust — adjacent to admin-control and exit-risk concerns. Collateral tiering and the curator-behavior alerts also speak to who-can-rug dynamics at the vault level.
- **Financial / market signal:** Dominant in volume. TVL, APR/APY, fee/revenue economics, liquidation/bad-debt history, capital-flow and AUM alerts — classic market/credit-health metrics.

**Orientation: mixed**, leaning financial-market with a distinctive curator-counterparty / operational overlay. Specific metrics: composite 0–100 score, High/Medium/Low grade, 9 hard-requirement flags, TVL, APR/APY, fee revenue, liquidations/bad debt, capital-flow and AUM anomaly alerts, curator legal-entity and AUM data.

It **complements** rather than duplicates the registry: BlockAnalitica covers quantitative lending-market risk broadly; DeFiScan covers decentralization/admin-control; Credora covers credit ratings. CuratorWatch is the only listed feed whose unit of analysis is the **Morpho-style vault curator** (the operator entity), making it the natural feed for Morpho / Morpho Vaults coverage and a unique angle (curator behavior, fee extraction, allocation risk) not provided elsewhere.

> Note for the aggregator: CuratorWatch produces its **own composite 0–100 score and letter grades**. Under RFP §1/§2 the aggregator must not generate composite scores, but it may display a provider's verbatim score. CuratorWatch's score is the provider's editorial output and is fine to surface verbatim; it must not be blended into any cross-feed synthesis.

## Inclusion assessment

- **Verdict:** include
- **Reasoning:**
  - *Access:* Fully public and free — no login, paywall, or API key observed across the homepage, docs, and lens pages. This makes it automatable and consistent with a public-good aggregator.
  - *Scope breadth:* Narrow by design, but it uniquely and deeply covers **Morpho + Morpho Vaults**, two of the Top-20 seed protocols, plus a possible partial reach into Aave/Euler/Compound/Spark via Turtle. Per the skill's coverage-relevance rule, a narrow feed that uniquely covers high-value Top-20 protocols still merits inclusion.
  - *Is it actually a risk feed?* Yes — it is a purpose-built vault/curator risk dashboard with explicit grading, risk factors, liquidation/bad-debt tracking, and anomaly alerts. Matches its RFP §4 "Dashboard" type.
  - *Neutrality & conflicts:* Operator/team identity is undisclosed on public surfaces — an open question, but no declared commercial ties to listed protocols were found.
  - *Redundancy:* Low. It is the only registry feed centered on the curator-as-operator unit, complementing the broader lending-risk and decentralization feeds.
  - *Coverage relevance:* High for Morpho/Morpho Vaults specifically — exactly where the curator model lives.

  Could be downgraded to `conditional` only if (a) coverage proves to be Morpho-only with no usable signal for other Top-20 protocols and the aggregator wants broader-reach feeds, or (b) the lack of a stable machine-readable surface makes scraping too brittle to automate reliably.

## How to fetch

- **Method: scrape.** No public, documented API, JSON endpoint, data export, subgraph, or GitHub repo was found. The site is a server-rendered web app (the changelog notes a migration to "Vercel Pro" for execution, consistent with a Next.js/Vercel deployment), so the practical route is HTML/Next.js data scraping of the public pages:
  - `https://curatorwatch.com/` — curators index + grades
  - `https://curatorwatch.com/yields` — yield lens
  - `https://curatorwatch.com/fees` — fee/revenue lens
  - `https://curatorwatch.com/liquidations` — liquidations / bad debt
  - `https://curatorwatch.com/alerts` — alert stream
  - `https://curatorwatch.com/docs` — methodology (grading rules, factor weights)
  - `https://curatorwatch.com/changelog` — feature/coverage history
- **Likely underlying data:** Next.js apps frequently expose hydration data via `__NEXT_DATA__` or `/_next/data/<buildId>/<route>.json` route payloads; if present these are far more robust scrape targets than HTML. This must be verified live later (out of scope for documentation-only research).
- **Auth / rate limits:** No auth required; no documented rate limits. Scrape politely.
- **Update cadence:** Data refreshes ~every 6h (hourly snapshot collection per docs); a once-daily scrape is sufficient to stay current.
- **Format:** HTML (likely with embedded JSON via Next.js).
